Quick Study Guide: Core Evaluation Criteria

Before you begin, keep these principles from the ACRL Framework Guide in mind:

  • Authority is Contextual: Expertise depends on the specific research question, not just a generic title.
  • Evidence as a Process: Look past surface claims to verify original methodologies and citations.
  • Relevance: Match the scope, population, and context of the source directly to your topic.
  • Currency: Consider whether your topic requires real-time updates or timeless historical context.

Read each scenario carefully, evaluate the sources, and choose the best response or sort them appropriately.
